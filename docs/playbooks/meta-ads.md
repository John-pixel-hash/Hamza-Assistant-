# Meta ads

## Account foundations, before spend
- [ ] Pixel and Conversions API installed, one real purchase or lead verified
      in Events Manager, not assumed.
- [ ] One custom conversion or event chosen as the optimization goal; never
      "traffic" for a store that needs sales.
- [ ] Domain verified and ad account in good standing; know the policy lines
      for the niche before writing copy.

## Structure that can be read
- One campaign per goal. One to three ad sets inside, split by audience only
  when budgets can feed each of them (50 conversions a week per ad set is the
  rough floor for learning).
- Creative is the targeting now. Broad audience plus strong creative beats
  stacked interest sets at small budgets.

## Creative testing framework
- Test angles, not tweaks: one offer angle, one objection angle, one social
  proof angle. Three creatives per angle minimum before judging.
- Kill rule: after 3 times the target cost per result with nothing, the
  creative dies. No sentiment.
- Winner gets budget moved to it, in 20 percent steps, every few days, never
  daily resets.

## Budget pacing
- Daily budget at the ad set or advantage level, steady. Big jumps reset
  learning; move in small steps.
- First week is learning money: agree with the client in writing that week
  one buys data, not profit.

## Diagnosis tree: no sales
1. Is the pixel firing a real purchase event? If not, fix tracking first; the
   ads are innocent until proven guilty.
2. Clicks but no add to cart: the page or the offer is the problem. Check
   price against the promise, mobile speed, and the first screen.
3. Add to cart but no purchase: shipping and checkout shock. Test the checkout
   yourself on a phone, to the last button.
4. No clicks at all: the creative is invisible. New hook, new first line.

## Diagnosis tree: high CPM
1. Audience too narrow or overlapping: broaden, merge ad sets.
2. Creative fatigue: frequency above 3 on the same people, refresh the hook.
3. Policy or quality flags: check account quality, rewrite claim heavy copy.

## Policy risk
- No before and after claims, no income promises, no "you" accusations in
  health or money niches. When unsure, write the safer version and test.

## Research refresh
- current Meta advantage plus defaults and what changed in campaign structure
- current CPM and CPA benchmarks for small ecommerce in the client's region

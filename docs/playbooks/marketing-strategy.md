# Digital marketing strategy

## Positioning before channels
- One sentence: who it is for, what it does, and the proof. If the client
  cannot say it, no channel will say it for them.
- The offer comes before the ads. A weak offer with great targeting is still
  a weak offer.

## Channel selection by budget and margin
- Under a few hundred a month: do not buy ads. Organic plus outreach plus
  email earn more per dollar; say so plainly instead of taking the ad budget.
- High margin, search demand exists: Google search ads first; the intent is
  already typed.
- Impulse or visual product, some budget to learn: Meta ads with a creative
  testing plan, and week one agreed as learning money.
- Owner comfortable on camera: short form organic as the long game, ads only
  to amplify what already proved itself.

## Funnel design for small businesses
- One funnel, one path: ad or post to one page, one action, one follow up.
  Every extra fork leaks money and clarity.
- The follow up is where small businesses win: a reply within the hour, an
  email the same day. Speed is the cheapest conversion tool they own.

## Measurement that is actually implementable
- One north star with money in it: booked calls, orders, deposits. Track it in
  one place the client opens weekly.
- Tracking stack as small as possible: pixel plus one sheet. A dashboard
  nobody opens is not measurement, it is decoration.
- Agree the report rhythm before launch: one page, weekly, three numbers and
  one decision. More than that dies in month two.

## Decision tree: "we need more followers"
1. Ask what followers have ever paid for. Move the goal to leads or sales.
2. If brand truly is the goal: pick one platform the owner enjoys, one format,
   three posts a week, reviewed monthly. Consistency over virality.
3. Never sell follower count as a service; it cannot be delivered honestly.

## Decision tree: budget cut in half
1. Keep the channel with tracked money; cut the one with vibes.
2. Inside the kept channel, cut breadth not depth: fewer ad sets, same learning floor.
3. Move the freed time to the free engines: reply speed, email, outreach.

## When to say no
- A channel the budget cannot feed, a niche with policy landmines the client
  will not respect, or a goal that is a feeling instead of a number. Say no
  with the reason and the alternative; that is the service.

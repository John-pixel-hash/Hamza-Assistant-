# Fiverr and client management

## Gig and inbox basics
- One gig, one promise, one buyer avatar. A gig that says everything sells nothing.
- First reply within the hour when awake; speed is the first trust signal on Fiverr.
- First message never opens with price. It opens with one sharp question about
  their project, then a short read back of what they need.

## Negotiation without discounting
- Never cut the price. Change the package instead: fewer pages, one revision
  round, longer delivery. The number stays; the scope moves.
- Anchor high first: quote the better package, then offer the smaller one as
  the budget option. Let them choose down, never beg down.
- A buyer pushing hard on price gets one counter, then silence. Chasing reads
  as weakness and kills the margin.

## Scope and revisions
- The order page is the contract. Anything outside it is a paid extra, offered
  politely with its price and days, before it is done.
- Revisions fix the delivered work against the brief; they are not a new
  project. A new direction is a new order.

## Review management
- Deliver one thing early (a first draft, a structure) to earn goodwill before
  the final ask.
- After delivery, one short message with the handover notes and a friendly
  nudge that a review helps. Never more than one nudge.
- A bad review threat is a scope dispute: move it to writing, restate what was
  agreed, offer one concrete fix with a date.

## Turning one offs into repeat clients
- At handover, name the next thing they will need (care plan, first ad campaign,
  email flow) with a price, in one line. Plant it; do not push it.
- Message past clients at their natural cycle: before their sale season, at
  renewal time. One line, no newsletter energy.

## Decision tree: buyer goes quiet mid order
1. One message with a single question and a date you will assume if silent.
2. Silent past the date: assume the answer, note it in writing, continue or
   pause, and say which.
3. Never send three chase messages; the third one loses the order and the review.

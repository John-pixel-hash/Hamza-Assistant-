# Google ads

## Before spend
- [ ] Conversion tracking verified with a real test conversion, imported from
      the tag plus enhanced conversions where possible.
- [ ] The landing page matches the promise of the keywords, on a phone.
- [ ] A negative keyword list started before launch, not after the burn.

## Search intent mapping
- Money keywords are the ones with buying intent: "buy", "near me", "price",
  "for hire", the service name plus the city. Informational words are blog
  traffic, not ad traffic.
- One ad group per tight intent. "Logo design" and "brand identity" are
  different buyers; mixing them blurs the copy and the quality score.

## Keyword and negative strategy
- Start exact and phrase match on the money terms; broad only after the
  search terms report shows clean behaviour for weeks.
- Read the search terms report weekly in month one. Every irrelevant click
  becomes a negative; that is the whole job of waste reduction.

## Ads and quality score
- Headline one repeats the keyword's promise; the landing page repeats the
  headline. Match is what quality score rewards.
- Two responsive search ads per ad group, different angles, let the data pick.

## Conversion tracking and bidding
- Track the thing with money in it: call, booking, purchase. "Traffic" as a
  goal teaches the algorithm to buy browsers, not buyers.
- Manual or target CPA once 30 conversions a month exist; before that, let it
  learn on maximize conversions with a honest cap.

## Waste reduction checklist
- [ ] Search terms reviewed, negatives added, weekly.
- [ ] Locations restricted to where the client actually serves.
- [ ] Schedule aligned to when the client can answer (no overnight burn for a
      one person business).
- [ ] Device split checked; if mobile converts at zero, bid down, do not guess.

## Diagnosis tree: spend but no conversions
1. Tracking first: test conversion end to end yourself.
2. Terms report: are real buyers clicking, or browsers and job seekers.
3. Landing page on a phone: does the first screen answer the keyword.
4. Price and response speed: call the client's number yourself; a dead phone
   kills ad money faster than bad ads.

## Research refresh
- current Google ads match type and broad match behaviour changes this year
- current average CPC benchmarks for the client's service and region

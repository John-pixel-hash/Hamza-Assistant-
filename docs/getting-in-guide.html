Getting into your app

Two ways. The first is shorter and I recommend it. Follow one only.

Way 1: create the account in Supabase Recommended

Skips ALLOW_SIGNUP completely, and skips the confirmation email step. About three minutes.

  1. Supabase, left sidebar Click Authentication, then Users.
  2. Top right of that page Click Add user and choose Create new user.
  3. In the form Type your email and a password of at least 12 characters. Type the password into your phone's password manager too, because this is the password you will sign in with.

    Turn Auto Confirm User ON. That is what avoids the confirmation email entirely. If you leave it off you will get stuck waiting for an email that Supabase's free sender often never delivers.

  4. Same page, click Create user Done. Your account exists. The database automatically creates your profile and all nine playbooks the moment the account appears.
  5. Vercel, a different tab Open Settings, then Environment Variables, and add one variable:

    Name: ALLOWED_EMAIL
    Value: the same email you just used
    Tick Production, Preview and Development, then Save.

  6. Vercel, top menu Open Deployments, click the ... menu on the newest one, and click Redeploy.

    This step is not optional. Vercel does not apply new variables to a deployment that already exists.

  7. Your site Open it and you will land on the sign in page. Enter the email and password you chose in step 3. You are in.

Way 2: use the app's own setup page

The original plan. It works, but it takes two environment changes and you have to wait for a confirmation email.

  1. Vercel, Settings, Environment Variables Find ALLOW_SIGNUP in the list. If it is there, click the ... on its row and choose Edit, then set the value to true. If it is not there, click Add New and create it.

    Exactly true. Lowercase, nothing else. Tick all three environments, then Save.

  2. Vercel, Deployments Redeploy the newest deployment.
  3. Your site, the address bar Go to /setup and create your account. 12 characters or more.
  4. Your inbox Supabase emails you a confirmation link. Click it. Check spam. If it never arrives, go back to Way 1 instead of waiting.
  5. Vercel, Environment Variables Now set ALLOWED_EMAIL to your address, change ALLOW_SIGNUP to false, and redeploy one more time.
Why the second environment change? While ALLOW_SIGNUP is true, the app is still ready to accept a new account. Turning it off, and naming your email in ALLOWED_EMAIL, is what makes the system private to you and refuses every other address. Way 1 gets both of those done in a single change.

What you should see afterwards

  • The sign in page stops showing the yellow warning.
  • Signing in lands you on Today, an empty dashboard.
  • Every page has a bottom bar on your phone. It works from anywhere.
